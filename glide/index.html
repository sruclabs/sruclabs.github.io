04FAQ

Asked
often.

Glide can't find my TV. What do I check?

First, confirm your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network — not a guest network, and not mobile data. Then check the TV is powered on with its network features enabled, and restart both the TV and the app before trying again.

How do I pair with my TV?

Pick your television from the discovery list. Some platforms connect directly; others show a code on the TV or ask for an on-screen confirmation. Follow the prompt on the television itself — Glide walks you through whichever your set needs.

Is my TV supported?

Glide supports Roku, LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Android TV, Fire TV, Vizio SmartCast, and Hisense VIDAA sets that accept network control. Support varies by model and software version — older sets may offer fewer controls. Apple TV and IR-only sets are not supported.

Do I need an account or a hub?

No. There is no account, no sign-in, and no hub hardware. Everything happens between your phone and your TV over your home network.

What is the difference between the universal remote and a platform deck?

The universal remote covers the shared essentials — navigation, power, volume, input. Switching to a platform deck adds the controls specific to that system. One app, both levels.

Can I control more than one TV?

Yes. Glide keeps your paired sets in one place — pick a television and its deck loads. Each set pairs once and stays remembered on your phone.
