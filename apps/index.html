Shipped · v1 Magnet Search / Android

One search,
several sources.

Type once, check open indexes and public archives together. Open a result straight in your torrent app, or save it for later. No account, no search log on a server.

  • On-device — bookmarks and history stay on the phone
  • Straight handoff — magnet opens in the app you already use

In work Glide / Android

One remote,
every television.

Glide turns the phone you already carry into the only remote the living room needs. Pair over your home network, then power, volume, and input from one quiet screen. Nothing leaves the house.

  • Local pairing — no hub, no account, no cloud
  • One screen — power, volume, input, D-pad
  • At rest — dims itself, sips battery
